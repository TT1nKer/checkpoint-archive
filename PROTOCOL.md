# Frozen pilot protocol

Question: can two temporal references reduce the total size of a bit-exact offline weight archive under a limit on the number of distinct snapshot records required for a cold restore? Each record is a whole version, with independently coded blocks. The primary limit is five records, including the requested version. Limits of three and eight are secondary. Record count and compressed read bytes are separate outcomes.

Use versions 32k–48k and 112k–128k, stride 1k, of the public Pythia-14m and Pythia-70m series. Use complete original 14m safetensors and fixed central slices from 13 tensors of the 70m files. Keep dtype and IEEE bits. No optimizer state. The four cohorts share a model family; the two phases of a model are related observations.

Use identical block codecs for every candidate. A single-parent record may reference any other snapshot, earlier or later. A dual-parent record may choose left, right or a time-linear prediction per block. Test centered temporal bridges of radius 1, 2, 4 or 8 versions. Also evaluate the same dual plan with interpolation disabled and a feasible one-parent collapse. Count archive metadata. Select layouts on a fixed stratified guide of eight contiguous 4096-element blocks, centered in eight equal-length regions of the flat payload. Model the original header overhead on the guide too. Rebuild selected plans on all available values in blocks of 262144 elements, measure complete file sizes and verify original snapshot bytes. Use the strongest implemented feasible single-parent archive on the full payload. Distinguish guide optimization from full-payload optimality.

Primary decision: continue substantial research only if savings reach 3% at K=5 in at least three cohorts, including a complete 14m window, with mean compressed cold-read bytes no higher on those successes. For a positive decision also check decoder cost and reproducibility. The threshold is an engineering investment decision, not a significance test. Report failures and sensitivity without retuning the predictors or thresholds.
